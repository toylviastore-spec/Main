1. Liquid lens

The page builds a displacement map in JavaScript. It models a rounded glass edge and bends light through it with Snell's law, then feeds the map to an SVG filter used as backdrop-filter. Drag the lens around.

2. Cards

Each card has a gradient ring border made with mask-composite, faint film grain, and a highlight that follows your pointer.

Clear

The ring is brightest top-left and glints bottom-right, like light on a real edge.

Tinted

A coloured fill gradient on the same base.

Dark

Best contrast for longer text on busy backgrounds.

3. Glass inside glass

An element with backdrop-filter becomes a backdrop root. Glass inside it only sees what the parent paints. The left chip loses its blur where it sticks out. On the right, the parent's blur sits on a pseudo-element, so the chip blurs the page correctly.
